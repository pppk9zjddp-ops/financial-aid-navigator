How to use it

Three steps, about two minutes

1

Say what you need

Tap a suggestion or type in your own words: bursaries, scholarships or both. Starting next year or already studying, citizenship, household size and a rough income range are all it asks for.

2

Answer a few questions

The Navigator asks only for what is still missing, in one message, and never twice. Then it checks verified SMU data: your per capita income, your bursary tier (same result as SMU's own calculator in 12 of 12 test cases) and 201 SMU scholarships.

3

Get your plan, then ask more

You see likely bursaries and scholarships with amounts, the reason each fits and official links. Follow up with documents, deadlines, a calendar reminder or an email draft for SMU's aid office.
